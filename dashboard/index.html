How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor × chain factor. The category multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of live-token protocols in the same category in this snapshot, winsorised to 0.005–2.0 and shrunk toward the market median with eight pseudo-observations, capped at 0.02–0.60. Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, weighted 0.35. Maturity rises from 0.70 to 1.00 over three years listed with no token; an unknown listing date counts as 365 days. Each extra chain adds 0.03, up to three.

Live-token protocols are shown separately, at the market's own values — never mixed into the ranked board. A protocol is ranked only if it records no token and is not an exchange, a bridge, a liquid-staking or restaking product, a versioned pool, or a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades. The snapshot records no token; it never proves a protocol has none.

Farm plans are built from each protocol's own category, chain, TVL and age. Amounts, gas and hold times are in every step. Never pay to claim an airdrop, and claim only from a link the protocol itself publishes.
